## The word

**hoverboss** — a portmanteau of *hover* and *boss*: the boss who hovers over your shoulder, watching every keystroke, never quite leaving your desk. Funny on its own, true of bosses everywhere, and it needs no caption to land.

## How it works

`main.py` writes exactly one word to `/output/entry.txt`:

```
hoverboss
```

No spaces, no sentence, no explanation — the word is the whole entry.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
